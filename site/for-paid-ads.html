FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Duct for paid ads?

An open-source AI agent that reads Google Ads, Meta Ads, Apple Search Ads, OpenAI Ads, GA4 and Stripe together. Ask why CPA moved and it answers across platforms with its sources; when the answer is a pause, a negative or a budget change, it proposes the change set and applies it only when you approve.

How long does it take to connect my ad platforms to Duct?

Under five minutes. Duct uses one-click OAuth to connect Google Ads, Meta Ads, Apple Search Ads, OpenAI Ads, GA4, Search Console, HubSpot, Stripe and RevenueCat. No API keys to configure and no developer time required. You select your platforms, authorize access, and your first daily ad intelligence brief arrives the next morning.

Does Duct modify anything in my ad accounts?

Not unless you approve it. Duct reads your performance data to build the brief, and reporting works with read-only access alone. When it finds something worth acting on — a wasted-spend search term to add as a negative keyword, a campaign to pause — it proposes the change, shows you every affected item before and after, and applies it only once you approve. Every applied change is logged and reversible where the platform allows it. OAuth tokens are encrypted and stored server-side, and all data in transit is encrypted via TLS.

How is Duct different from building cross-platform ad reports in spreadsheets or dashboards?

Spreadsheets and dashboards show you what happened — Duct tells you what to do about it. Instead of manually pulling CSVs from three platforms and merging them weekly, Duct automatically correlates data across Google Ads, Meta, and LinkedIn to surface creative fatigue, budget reallocation opportunities, and cross-channel attribution gaps that static reports miss entirely.

How much does Duct for paid ads cost?

Free with your own keys, always. Duct is MIT licensed: you pay your model provider directly, or sign in with the ChatGPT plan you already have. A paid plan later bundles the models for teams that would rather hold no keys; nothing open today moves behind it. What will cost money is spelled out on the open-source page.

What kind of changes can it make?

Pausing a campaign, adding negative keywords, and moving a budget within the guardrail you set for the account. Each arrives as a change set with a preview; destructive changes are flagged, anything over a guardrail is blocked until a person looks, and every applied change can be rolled back.
