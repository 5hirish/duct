FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Duct for organic growth?

Duct connects Search Console, Ahrefs, Semrush, GA4, and content tools into one weekly action brief. It tells growth teams exactly which pages to update, which keywords to target, and what to publish next.

How does Duct connect to my SEO and content tools?

Duct connects to Search Console, Ahrefs, GA4, Semrush, Notion, LinkedIn Analytics, HubSpot, Slack, and Mixpanel via one-click OAuth. Setup takes under five minutes and your first weekly brief lands in your inbox after connecting.

Does Duct store my SEO data or share it with third parties?

No. Duct uses official APIs in strict read-only mode and never stores data without your approval. Data in transit is encrypted via TLS and OAuth tokens are stored server-side.

How is Duct different from using Ahrefs or Search Console directly?

SEO tools show data in isolation. Duct correlates ranking, traffic, and conversion signals across tools and surfaces prioritized actions instead of disconnected dashboards.

What does Duct for organic growth cost?

Duct is free during beta with no credit card required. We onboard teams hands-on, then announce paid plans after beta with early adopter pricing.

What kind of actions does the weekly organic brief recommend?

Each week, Duct highlights pages losing rankings, keyword gaps, high-opportunity content briefs, and anomaly alerts. Actions are ranked by projected impact using cross-tool signals.
