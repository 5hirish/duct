FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Duct for organic growth?

An open-source AI agent that reads Google Search Console, GA4, Tag Manager, Microsoft Clarity and Mixpanel together. Ask which content is converting and it answers with the pages to update and the queries to target; a free SEO audit runs on any URL without an account; and Content Studio plans, drafts and publishes the week's posts.

How does Duct connect to my SEO and content tools?

Duct connects to Google Search Console, GA4, Google Tag Manager, Microsoft Clarity, Mixpanel, HubSpot, Google Ads and Stripe via one-click OAuth. Setup takes under five minutes and your first weekly brief lands in your inbox after connecting.

Does Duct store my SEO data or share it with third parties?

Duct stores the reports it generates for you and the data needed to produce them, so briefs stay comparable week to week. It never sells your data or shares it with third parties for their own purposes. Data in transit is encrypted via TLS, OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest and never exposed to the browser, and you can delete your data at any time.

How is Duct different from using Search Console directly?

Search Console shows you queries and positions in isolation. Duct correlates ranking, traffic, behaviour and conversion signals across Search Console, GA4, Clarity and Mixpanel, then surfaces prioritised actions instead of disconnected dashboards.

What does Duct for organic growth cost?

Free with your own keys, always. Duct is MIT licensed: you pay your model provider directly, or sign in with the ChatGPT plan you already have. A paid plan later bundles the models for teams that would rather hold no keys; nothing open today moves behind it. What will cost money is spelled out on the open-source page.

What kind of actions does Duct recommend?

Pages losing rankings and the query they lost, clusters that earn trials versus clusters that only earn traffic, technical fixes from the audit in priority order, and the posts worth making next. Each one names its sources, and when the action is a change in a connected tool, it arrives as a change set you approve.
