Frequently asked questions

Is this actually free?

Duct crawls your sitemap, reads your pages, and runs them through an AI-powered SEO analysis pipeline. It checks technical signals (meta tags, redirects, TTFB), on-page signals (titles, headings, content), and surface keyword and content gaps.

How long does it take?

Most audits complete in 2–4 minutes, depending on the size of your site. You'll see a live progress indicator while it runs.

What happens after the audit?

You get the full interactive report — scores, findings, and a prioritized action plan. You can download it, share it, and optionally sign up to save it and run follow-up audits.
