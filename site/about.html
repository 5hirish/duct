What Duct Does

Connect everything.
Understand everything.

Duct reads data from your advertising, analytics, support, SEO, and project management tools — then delivers structured intelligence briefs with cross-tool signals and clear next actions.

📊

Cross-tool reports

On-demand and scheduled reports that pull data from every connected tool. Campaign performance, product metrics, search rankings, support trends — correlated in one view.

⚡

Anomaly detection

When something spikes, drops, or breaks pattern, Duct flags it immediately and cross-references other tools to explain why — before you even notice.

💬

Plain-English synthesis

No more decoding dashboards at 8am. Duct delivers narrative briefs that explain what happened, what it means, and what to do next — in language your whole team understands.

How We Work

Principles, not platitudes

Read-only by default

We look, never touch

Duct reads your tools to build its briefs. It changes nothing in your accounts unless you approve a specific change first — and it shows you exactly what will change before you do. Your data, your control — always.

Substance over ceremony

Ship the insight, skip the noise

We don’t build features to pad a changelog. Every report, every signal, every alert exists because it saves a real team real time on a real decision.

Underdog energy

Small enough to care, fast enough to matter

We’re not backed by a megacorp or staffed by hundreds. We’re a tiny crew that builds with urgency, listens obsessively, and ships every single week. That’s the advantage.

Transparent by design

You see what we see

Every data source, every API call, every piece of logic that generates a signal — it’s documented and inspectable. No black boxes, no “trust us” handwaving.
