Built because my tools wouldn't talk.
Open because it reads all of them.

I got tired of carrying numbers between tools by hand, so I built the agent that reads them together. It is MIT licensed, because something that reads your whole business should be readable back.

FONS, the spring. The app’s front door.

Every tool sees a slice. Nobody reads them together.

  1. The problem

    Analytics knows what happened, the ad platform what you spent, Stripe who paid. Once a week someone carries the numbers between them by hand.

  2. So I connected them

    Duct reads them together and answers why a number moved. When the answer is a change, it prepares it and waits for your yes.

  3. Then I opened it

    Your ads and revenue are a lot to trust one person's tool with. So every prompt, every API call and the approval gate are on GitHub. What runs where

Three agents today.

  • InsightsAsk why signups dropped. It reads Google Ads, GA4 and Stripe together, and a second agent re-checks every number.
  • SEO auditPaste a URL and get the fixes in order. Free, no account.
  • Content StudioPlan posts on a board and publish to nine networks. The results come back to each card.

A duct is not a source.
It’s what carries.

Duct is Latin, ductus: a leading. It sits inside aquaeductus, water led. Rome’s aqueducts made no water. They carried it, on gravity alone, to where people lived.

The Nîmes aqueduct fell about twelve metres over fifty kilometres, a slope you could not see standing next to it. Your data is the spring. Duct is the channel to where the decision gets made.
