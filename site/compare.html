FAQ

Before you choose

Is Duct a Claude Cowork alternative?

For one job, yes: finding why your growth numbers moved and making the change inside your guardrails, with a rollback. For everything else Cowork does, from documents to browsing the web, no, and it does not try to be. Running both is reasonable.

Can n8n do what Duct does?

Much of it, if you build it. n8n can pull Google Ads, GA4 and Stripe data, put an AI agent on it, and hold its tool calls for an approval in Slack. Duct ships that as the product: the connectors, the joins, the change preview, the guardrails and the rollback, with nothing to wire.

Is n8n open source?

n8n is fair-code under its Sustainable Use License. The source is public and self-hosting for your own business is free, but commercial resale is restricted, so it is not open source in the OSI sense. Duct is MIT licensed.

Which one is cheapest?

Duct is free with your own model keys or your ChatGPT plan; you pay the model provider for what you use. n8n is free to self-host, and its cloud starts at 20 euros a month billed annually. Cowork comes with a paid Claude plan, from 20 dollars a month.
