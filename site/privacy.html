Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 5, 2026

Alleviate Lab ("we", "us", "our") operates Duct (getduct.ai), an automated intelligence platform that connects your advertising, analytics, and search tools to deliver actionable reports. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you sign in with Google, we receive:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your Google profile picture

We use this information solely to identify your account and personalize your experience within Duct.

Connected Platform Data

When you connect data sources (e.g., Google Ads), we access your data through official APIs in read-only mode. Specifically:

  • Google Ads: Campaign performance metrics (clicks, impressions, cost, conversions, ROAS), search terms, device and geographic segmentation, and ad group data

We do not create, modify, or delete any data in your connected platforms. Access is strictly read-only.

Usage Data

We collect basic usage analytics via Google Tag Manager to understand how people use our product. This may include pages visited, features used, and general interaction patterns. We do not track individual keystrokes or form inputs beyond what is needed for the product to function.

Locally Stored Data

We store an authentication token in your browser's local storage to keep you signed in. This token contains your name, email, and profile picture, and expires after 7 days.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Authentication: To verify your identity and maintain your session
  • Report generation: To fetch data from your connected platforms and generate intelligence reports
  • Product improvement: Aggregated, anonymized usage patterns help us improve the product

3. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes
  • We do not store your Google Ads performance data permanently beyond the scope of generating your requested reports
  • We do not write to, modify, or manage your ad accounts. Duct is read-only
  • We do not use your data to train machine learning models

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google OAuth: For secure sign-in authentication
  • Google Ads API: For reading your advertising performance data (read-only)
  • Google Tag Manager: For basic product analytics
  • Cloudflare: For hosting, CDN, and bot protection (Turnstile)

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them.

5. Data Security

  • All data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS
  • OAuth tokens are stored server-side and never exposed to the browser
  • API routes are protected with API key authentication
  • We follow the principle of least privilege for all data access

6. Data Retention

  • Authentication tokens: Expire after 7 days
  • Generated reports: Retained while your account is active
  • Raw platform data: Fetched on demand and not retained beyond the report generation process

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Delete your account and associated data by contacting us
  • Revoke connected platform access at any time through your Google Account permissions
  • Opt out of analytics tracking by using browser-level privacy tools or ad blockers

8. Beta Status

Duct is currently in beta. During this period, features and data handling practices may evolve. We will update this policy as needed and notify users of material changes via the email associated with their account.

9. Children's Privacy

Duct is not intended for use by individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
