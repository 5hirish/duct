FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Duct?

Duct is a cross-tool intelligence platform that connects your product, marketing, and revenue tools into one decision-ready brief. It shows what changed, why it matters, and what to do next without dashboard-hopping.

How long does setup take?

Most teams connect their first tools in about 10 minutes. Duct uses OAuth-based connections, so you can start quickly without custom integrations or manual exports.

Which tools does Duct connect to?

Duct connects to tools like GA4, Google Ads, Mixpanel, Intercom, HubSpot, Salesforce, Ahrefs, Hotjar, Slack, and more. The platform is built to correlate signals across tools instead of reporting each one in isolation.

How often do I receive briefs?

Duct delivers weekly intelligence briefs to your inbox and can send real-time alerts when important metrics spike or break. This gives teams both a strategic weekly view and faster operational visibility.

Does Duct modify my tools or data?

No. Duct operates in read-only mode and does not create, edit, or delete data in your connected platforms. It focuses on analysis and recommendations so your team can make faster decisions.

How is Duct different from dashboards?

Dashboards show isolated metrics. Duct connects cross-tool signals and ranks actions by business impact, so teams know which decision to make next instead of manually stitching reports.

Is Duct free during beta?

Yes. Duct is free during beta with no credit card required. Teams can join early access, connect their stack, and receive intelligence briefs while pricing evolves post-beta.
