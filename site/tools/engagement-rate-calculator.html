Questions about engagement rate

Followers, reach or impressions: which should I use?

Followers to compare accounts. Reach to compare posts that reached different numbers of people. Impressions when people saw the same post more than once.

What is a good engagement rate?

It varies by platform, format and audience size, and no one benchmark covers them all. The colors in the results (under 1% low, 6% and up strong) are a rough guide. Your own rate last month is the better yardstick.

What counts as an engagement?

Likes, comments, shares, saves, clicks and replies, or whatever your platform reports. Use the same definition every week so the trend means something.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. The math runs in your browser. Nothing is sent or stored.
