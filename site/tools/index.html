FAQ

About these tools

Do I need an account?

No. Every tool on this page runs in your browser. The Duct app is a separate download, free with your own keys, and these stay free either way.

How is this related to Duct the product?

Same team. A tool answers one question with the numbers you type in. Duct answers it with the numbers in your tools, every time you ask, and proposes what to change.

Will you store my inputs?

No. The maths runs in your browser and nothing you type leaves it.
