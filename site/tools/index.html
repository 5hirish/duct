FAQ

About these tools

Do I need an account?

No. Every calculator and generator on this page runs in your browser without a Duct login. If you join the beta later, that is separate — these stay free.

How is this related to Duct the product?

The same team builds both. These tools help you answer one-off questions quickly; Duct answers them continuously by connecting your product, marketing, and revenue stack into weekly briefs and alerts.

Will you store my inputs?

Calculations happen locally in your session — we do not persist your numbers on our servers for these free tools. For Duct beta, connection and brief delivery work under our privacy policy.
