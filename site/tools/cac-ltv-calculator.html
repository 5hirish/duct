Questions about CAC and LTV

What goes into CAC?

Everything you spent to win those customers in the same period: ad spend, tools, agency fees, and the salaries of the people doing sales and marketing.

Can I work out LTV from churn?

Yes. Months they stay is 1 ÷ monthly churn, so LTV is revenue per customer ÷ churn. At 120 a month and 2.5% churn, that is 120 ÷ 0.025 = 4,800.

Why does payback ask for gross margin?

Revenue isn't profit. With a margin, payback counts the months until gross profit covers CAC, which is the slower and truer number.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. The math runs in your browser. Nothing is sent or stored.
