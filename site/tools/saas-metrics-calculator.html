Questions about SaaS metrics

Should churn be customers or revenue?

Either, as long as you use the same one every month. Revenue churn suits LTV better when your customers pay very different amounts.

Why is LTV estimated from churn?

It is the standard shortcut, and it assumes churn stays flat and customers never upgrade. Enter your own LTV if you have a better figure.

Where do the benchmarks come from?

Churn from ChartMogul's SaaS Benchmarks Report (2024), payback and trial-to-paid from OpenView's SaaS Benchmarks (2023–2024), and the 3:1 LTV:CAC rule from David Skok's SaaS metrics framework.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. The math runs in your browser. Nothing is sent or stored.
