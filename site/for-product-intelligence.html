FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Duct for product intelligence?

Duct connects Mixpanel, Microsoft Clarity, GrowthBook, GA4 and HubSpot into one daily PM brief. It surfaces cross-tool correlations, detects anomalies, and answers plain-English questions without SQL.

What tools does Duct connect to, and how hard is setup?

Duct connects to Mixpanel, GA4, Microsoft Clarity, GrowthBook, HubSpot, Stripe, RevenueCat and Google Ads with one-click OAuth. Setup takes under five minutes and your first daily PM briefing arrives in your inbox after connecting.

Is my product data safe with Duct?

Your data is encrypted in transit via TLS, and OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest and never exposed to the browser. Duct reads your tools to build the brief and changes nothing in them unless you approve a specific change first. Access is scoped to the project members you invite, and you can disconnect a source or delete your data at any time.

How is Duct different from using Mixpanel dashboards directly?

Single-tool dashboards show isolated metrics. Duct correlates product analytics, session behaviour, experiment results and revenue signals so you can prioritise what to ship next without manual correlation.

Does Duct for product intelligence cost anything?

Duct is free during beta with no credit card required. Teams get hands-on onboarding, and paid pricing will be announced after beta with early adopter rates.

What does the daily PM briefing actually contain?

The daily briefing highlights changes across activation, retention, support volume, issue flow, and replay patterns, then explains what is blocking adoption and what to ship next.
