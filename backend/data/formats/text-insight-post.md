# Text Insight Post — a hook, a story, a lesson, a question

**The format for words-first feeds.** Text is still the top-engagement format on X, and on LinkedIn a plain post with a strong first line outperforms most images. What the feeds reward is the first line (it decides the click on "see more") and the comments (a question at the end is the cheapest engagement there is).

---

## Philosophy

One insight per post, earned by one concrete story. The reader should recognise themselves in the first two lines, learn one thing by the middle, and have something to say by the end. It sounds like a person typing, not a brand publishing.

Short lines. White space. No formatting tricks the platform does not support.

---

## Structure

| Part | Lines | What it does |
|------|-------|--------------|
| Hook | 1 (≤ 12 words) | A claim, a confession or a number that stops the scroll. Stands alone before "see more". |
| Turn | 1 | The line that makes the hook surprising: "and it was my fault", "it took three years", "nobody noticed". |
| Story | 3–6 short lines | What happened, concretely: who, when, what changed. One scene, not a biography. |
| Lesson | 1–2 | The insight, stated plainly. The line people quote. |
| Question | 1 | One open question the reader can answer in a sentence. Not "thoughts?". |

Each part is its own paragraph. One sentence per line on LinkedIn; on X the whole thing is often the hook, the lesson and the question in three lines.

---

## Per-channel shape

| Channel | Length | Shape |
|---------|--------|-------|
| LinkedIn | 600–1,300 characters | Full structure. First 210 characters show before "see more": the hook and the turn live there. One blank line between every line. A first comment carries any link. |
| X | ≤ 280 characters, or a thread | One insight: hook + lesson + question. A story goes in a thread of 3–5 posts, each one complete on its own; the first post is the hook alone. Links in a reply, not the post. |
| Threads | ≤ 500 characters | Conversational. Hook + story in two short paragraphs + question. Replies continue it. |
| Bluesky | ≤ 300 characters | As X. Threads are read in full, so a 3-post story works. |

The channel's hard limit is on the post (`post.channel`); the writer refuses a draft over it rather than trimming after.

---

## Hook patterns

- The confession: "I lost our biggest client in a single email."
- The number: "47 cold emails. 1 reply. Here is what the reply said."
- The contrarian: "Your onboarding is too short, not too long."
- The small observation: "Every good PM I know does one strange thing in meetings."
- The time marker: "Three years ago I would have argued the opposite."

Avoid: a hook that starts with "I'm excited to", a question the reader can skip, a hook that names the product.

---

## Voice

- First person, past tense for the story, present tense for the lesson.
- Plain words. No "leverage", "unlock", "game-changer", "journey".
- One idea per line; a line is never longer than two short sentences.
- No emoji bullets. At most one emoji in the whole post, if the account uses them at all.
- Name real things: the tool, the number, the day of the week. Vagueness reads as fiction.
- The project's banned words and voice rules apply before any of this.

---

## Formatting

- No markdown. The feeds render none of it.
- No hashtags on LinkedIn, or at most three at the very end. None on Threads. One or two on X only when they are a real conversation.
- No link in the post body on LinkedIn or X; the first comment or reply carries it.
- Line breaks are structure. A wall of text loses the reader at "see more".

---

## What makes this format fail

- An announcement dressed as an insight ("We just shipped…").
- A hook longer than one line, or a hook that is only interesting after the second line.
- A story with no scene: "I learned a lot in my career" is not a story.
- A lesson that is a platitude ("consistency matters").
- "Thoughts?" as the question.
- A link in the body, which the feeds demote.
- Markdown bold or headers that render as literal asterisks.
- Three insights in one post.
- Over the channel's character limit.
