# Short Video Script — hook in three seconds, pay off in thirty

**The reach format.** Video is the top-engagement format on TikTok, Threads and Bluesky and the one every feed pushes to people who do not follow you yet. The feeds rank it on watch time: completions and replays. Every line of this recipe is about not losing the viewer.

---

## Philosophy

A short video is one idea delivered as a small story: a hook that makes a promise, a middle that pays it, and an ending that makes the next video more likely to be seen. Spoken words carry it. On-screen text repeats the key line so it works muted. The picture changes every few seconds so the thumb never gets bored.

Write the script before anything else. The shot list follows the script, never the other way round.

---

## Length

| Target | When |
|--------|------|
| 6–12 s | One visual payoff, a reaction, a single tip. Loops well. |
| 15–30 s | **Default.** One idea with a hook, two beats and an ending. |
| 30–60 s | Only when a demonstration needs it. Every extra 10 s has to earn its place with a new beat. |

Under 30 seconds completion rates stay high; past that, cut or split into a series.

---

## Script structure

| Time | Beat | What it does | On-screen text |
|------|------|--------------|----------------|
| 0–3 s | Hook | The promise, the problem or the surprising claim, spoken in the first second. Visual is already moving. | The hook, in ≤ 6 words, `cap-stroke` |
| 3–8 s | Setup | Why the viewer should care. One sentence. Raise the question. | None, or a 2-word label |
| 8–20 s | Payoff | The answer, the demonstration, the reveal. Two beats maximum, one cut between them. | The key line, `cap-pill` |
| 20–27 s | Turn | A detail they did not expect, a caveat, a second example. The replay reason. | Optional |
| 27–30 s | Ending | A reason to follow or a question for the comments. No "like and subscribe". Cut hard; no fade. | The question, `cap-raw` |

Write it as:

```
HOOK (0–3s) · [shot] · "spoken line" · [text on screen]
SETUP (3–8s) · ...
```

One line per beat. The shot in brackets, the spoken words in quotes, the on-screen text in square brackets.

---

## Hook rules

- Spoken in the first second, not after a greeting. Never "Hey guys".
- Starts mid-action: the thing is already happening when the video starts.
- Promise a specific payoff: a number, a result, a before/after.
- Patterns that work: "I was today years old when…", "Stop doing X", "Nobody tells you this about…", "The real reason…", "Watch what happens when…"
- The on-screen hook text and the spoken hook say the same thing in different words.

---

## Visual rules

- A cut or a camera move at least every 3 seconds. Static talking head for 30 s loses the thumb.
- Vertical 9:16, subject in the upper two thirds, text clear of the right 180 px and bottom 300 px.
- Face to camera for the hook and the ending; cutaways, screen recordings or B-roll for the payoff.
- Captions on by default. Burned-in captions use the style registry classes; auto-captions are the fallback.
- Natural light, phone-shot, slightly imperfect. Studio polish lowers trust on these feeds.

---

## Audio

- Voice first. A trending sound only underneath, at 20% or less.
- No music under the hook if the hook is spoken.
- Silence is allowed for one beat before the payoff.

---

## Caption (below the video)

- One line that adds what the video did not say, or asks the ending's question again.
- Three to five specific hashtags on TikTok and Instagram; none on Threads.
- No links in the caption on TikTok (they do not resolve); use the profile link.

---

## Image and clip prompt rules (generated footage)

When the video is assembled from generated clips rather than shot:

- One clip per beat, 3–8 seconds each, the same subject and light across clips.
- Describe motion, not a still: "camera pushes in slowly", "hands enter frame and open the box".
- Keep the lower third quiet for captions.
- No text, logos or UI in the generated clip.
- Realistic faces are refused by some models; use stylised or hands-only shots when the model declines.

---

## What makes this format fail

- A greeting before the hook. The first second is gone.
- A hook that is a topic, not a promise.
- Three ideas in thirty seconds; or one idea stretched to sixty.
- No cut for more than five seconds.
- The payoff at the very end — viewers leave before it arrives.
- Ending on "like and follow for more". End on a question or a cliff.
- A fade-out. Cut hard so the loop restarts cleanly.
- Music louder than the voice.
- Text behind the TikTok buttons or caption bar.
