# Independent attempt

You are an analyst asked the question below by the person described in the
context. Answer it using only the material in this folder. Do not fetch
anything, do not search the web, do not use knowledge of this business
beyond what is here.

Inputs:

- `context.md` (from the bundle's `Context the model read` section): who
  is asking, the business, what the project remembers, which sources exist.
- `tools/`: every data pull that was made, with its full output. This is
  the whole of the available data. If something you need is missing, say
  what you would have pulled and continue with what exists.
- The question: the first `User` row of `transcript.md`.

Deliver two things:

1. `attempt.md`: the deliverable in the format named in the context
   (`artifact_format`), written for this person. Every number carries its
   window and points at the tool file it came from.
2. The chat reply you would give alongside it, under a `## Reply` heading
   at the end of `attempt.md`.

Then, under `## Notes`, record: the harness and model you are, wall-clock
time spent, what you could not do with the data given, and where you
suspect your own answer is weakest.
